I heard tale long ago of a man with cancer who was given 6 months to live. He decided to go out in a blaze of laughter and locked himself up in a hotel room with jokebooks / comedians and friends - making the most of his time. 6 months later he hadnt kicked it and eventually was cured.

<a title="Google: Laughing Cures Cancer" href="http://www.google.com/search?hl=en&amp;q=laughing+cures+cancer&amp;btnG=Search">Googling</a> showed me <a title="laughing cures cancer" href="http://killcancercell.com/cancerstory/2008/07/cancer-cure-with-laughing/">this article</a> which proves that, though the details may be sketchy, so I most certainly am not making a mistake when I say this.

How, we ask, can this happen - <a title="Google: Endorphin" href="http://www.google.com/search?hl=en&amp;q=endorphins&amp;btnG=Search">endorphins</a> <a title="Wiki: Endorphin" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Endorphin">are the key</a>.<!--more-->

When asking does the end justify the means, I say to myself, if this thing cures cancer - bloody hell im gonna laugh any which way I can - slapstick / dry corn / corn on butter / dumbass jokes / <a title="Wiki: Sexual Innuendo" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Double_entendre">sexual and non</a> <a title="Wiki: Innuendo" href="en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Innuendo">innuendoes</a>.

I'd like to go the whole gamut - laughing / crying / swearing. I dont care which I do as long as its almost 24 / 7 / 365 / 100. If these things keep death at bay - who says I cant live a 100 years.

I say wit is the key. In this, <a title="Home: George Carlin" href="http://www.georgecarlin.com/">George</a> <a title="Wiki: George Carlin" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Carlin">Carlin</a> - , the jester-philosopher-poet, reigns supreme.
