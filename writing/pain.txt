Is when people you care about hurt themselves. Day in and day out. <a href="http://www.lyricsmode.com/lyrics/o/oasis/fucking_in_the_bushes.html">Where</a> was Moses when god said Love Thyself?

Is watching a dog cower and bolt away when you go near it for fear of humans.

Is wanting to watch the sun rise, and having no one to share it with. Or feeling lazy. Or both.

Is when people put themselves out of your reach. And when you mean well. Sometimes its fear of reproach, lack of courage, ignorance, misunderstanding or just bad timing. Why do living creatures do that?

Is knowing that all your life, you've been looking in all the wrong places.

Is knowing that God has a divine plan, he just hasn't told you what yet.

Is watching people in their ignorance, tread over everything you hold sacred, <a href="http://www.nasonart.com/personal/lifelessons/fountainhead.html">violating it</a>.

Is to wake up one day and find... No one told you when to run, you missed the <a href="http://lyrics.wikia.com/Pink_Floyd:Time">starting gun</a>.

Is when you watch a <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peace_on_Earth_(film)">cartoon</a> and have to laugh, because otherwise, you'll cry.
