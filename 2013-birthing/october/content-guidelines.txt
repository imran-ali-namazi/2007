Anyone is welcome to contribute to any of the various areas.

We map our content to the type of user you are (Parent, Philosopher, Artist) since this helps streamline the usage and lets you browse *anonymously* with maximum benefit.

There are a few considerations and these are:
<ul>
<li><b>Queen's English</b> We will use the Queen's English. The reason for this decision is that many of the works we plan to have will be from Old English and from the Commonwealth. Also, just to get a little [link type=post id=150] counter-culture vibe[/link] going.</li><!--more-->
<li><b>Good Language</b> Language will not be trashy / slangy. If you have no time to learn the nuances of the language and the culture we are trying to preserve, maybe you should come back when you've grown up a little.</li>
<li><b>Selectiveness</b> Were not trying to do an *include all*, so we will be quite selective in the artefacts we choose. Final Authority will rest with our Chief Curator.</li>
<li><b>Negative Ommisssion</b> Ignore the negative stuff. We dont mean whats distasteful to the individual, but whats not in keeping with our wholistic growth philosophy.</li>
<!--li><b></b></li-->
</ul>

Once you become a community member, we encourage you to do one of the following
<ul>
<li>Add artefacts to Space, Follow, Stories, Gists and create Mashups in your Speak profiles.</li>
<li>Contribute questions to Prescriptions, but try and ensure that the answer refers to an Artefact.</li>
<li>We encourage you to create Speak profiles to accelerate your growth as an individual as you find the right response from our community, or to make Growth profiles so we can find the right mentors / channels for you.</li>
<li>Volunteer to convert / upload works into Biblios, or just involve yourself in some activity in Work.</li>
</ul>
